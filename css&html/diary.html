My First Day

Dear Diary

Second year na ako. Hindi na ako yung lost freshman na hindi alam kung saan pupunta. Kabisa ko na halos yung campus, alam ko na rin kung saan tatambay at saan mura kumain 😅 Pero kahit sanay na ako sa environment, hindi pa rin madali. Mas mabigat na yung subjects ngayon, mas mataas expectations. Parang hindi ka na beginner, pero hindi ka pa rin expert. Though it was nice seeing your friends/classmate and excited at the same time. Im looking forward to new learnings, that im surely kinda enjoy.

Routine but Surviving

Dear Diary,

May routine na ako—gising, pasok, kain, gawa ng tasks, uwi, repeat. Minsan nakakapagod kasi parang paulit-ulit, pero at least hindi na ako nangangapa tulad before. Alam ko na paano i-handle oras ko (kahit minsan crammer pa rin 😭). Survival mode, pero mas controlled na.

Network Struggle

Dear Diary,

Grabe yung Networking namin. Akala ko simple lang siya—like internet lang ganon—but ang dami palang concepts. IP addresses, subnetting, protocols… minsan parang lutang na ako habang nagtuturo si sir 😭 May times na sinusulat ko lang lahat pero hindi ko agad gets. Kailangan ko pa ulit basahin pag-uwi. Nakaka-frustrate, pero hindi pwedeng sumuko kasi major subject siya.

Baby Thesis

Dear Diary,

Yung akala kong simple project lang sa InfoMan… naging baby thesis pala 😭 Kailangan namin gumawa ng system, from planning hanggang execution. Hindi lang basta gawa—kailangan may documentation, may explanation, at dapat logical lahat. Ang hirap lalo na sa database part. Yung paggawa ng ER diagram, tables, at relationships—nakakalito minsan. May times na parang okay na yung gawa namin, tapos biglang may mali pala. Nakaka-frustrate, pero at the same time, ang fulfilling kapag unti-unti mo siyang naiintindihan.

Filming Day

Dear Diary,

We had our film activity in Intramuros, and it turned out to be our final exam. Nakakaba at first kasi hindi lang siya basta project—kailangan namin galingan dahil final na siya. Yung location itself added pressure, pero at the same time, ang ganda rin ng vibe habang nagfi-film kami. Medyo nahirapan kami sa concepts at pagkuha ng shots, lalo na paulit-ulit yung takes at pagod na rin kami. Pero habang tumatagal, naging enjoyable din siya, lalo na kasama yung group ko. In the end, kahit nakakapagod, naging memorable siya. Hindi ko in-expect na yung final exam namin would be something creative and outside the classroom. It felt stressful, pero fulfilling din.

Filming Day

Dear Diary,

Tonight was our year-end party, and it felt like a reward for everything we’ve been through. The music, the laughter, the photos—we were all just living in the moment. For a few hours, deadlines and stress didn’t exist. I looked around and realized how much has changed since day one. We’re not the same nervous freshmen anymore.
